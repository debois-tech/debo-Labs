# Code of Conduct

Debo Labs is a place to learn. We want it welcoming to beginners and to people from every background.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Everyone here started by not knowing this stuff.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain the why.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, or sexual attention or imagery.
- No spam, and no publishing other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban anyone who breaks these
rules. To report a problem, use the repository's **Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"** (a private channel to the
maintainers) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
